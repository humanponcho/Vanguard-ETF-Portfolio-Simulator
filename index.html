Methodology and Assumptions

Monte Carlo Simulation

The simulator runs N independent annual return paths. Each year's return is drawn from a normal distribution using the Box-Muller transform, parameterised by the portfolio's blended expected return and volatility. Monthly contributions are added annually, net of transaction costs.

Fee Deductions

Expense ratios are deducted from each asset's gross return before blending. A platform fee of 0.25% p.a. is then deducted from the blended portfolio return. Transaction costs of 0.10% are applied to each monthly contribution.

Dividend Withholding Tax

VUCP and VUTY are distributing ETFs. The withholding rate you set is applied as a flat reduction to their gross returns before blending (e.g. a 15% withholding rate reduces VUCP's 6.0% gross return to 5.1% net). VUAG (accumulating) is unaffected.

Capital Gains Tax

CGT is estimated at the flat rate you specify, applied to simulated gains above total contributions at the end of the horizon (median outcome). No ISA wrappers, annual CGT allowances, or tax-loss harvesting are modelled.

Inflation Adjustment

Inflation is applied as a post-hoc deflator to the displayed annualised real returns. Contributions are not inflation-adjusted in the simulation itself.

Percentile Overlay Lines

The three coloured overlay lines are analytical growth curves, not sampled paths. The median uses the net portfolio return compounded annually. The 95th/5th lines use µ ± 1.645σ/√T as a first-order approximation of the expected return distribution. The results table percentiles are computed directly from simulated final values.

Limitations

  • Asset returns are modelled as independent — no correlation matrix is applied.
  • Returns are normally distributed — fat tails and tail risk are underestimated.
  • No sequence-of-returns risk on withdrawals is modelled.
  • Tax estimates are simplified flat-rate calculations and do not constitute financial advice.

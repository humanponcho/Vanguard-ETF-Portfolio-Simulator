Currency Risk Note

This portfolio includes ETFs priced in USD but held in GBP. Returns may fluctuate based on GBP/USD exchange rates. A weaker GBP increases portfolio value, while a stronger GBP reduces it (all else equal).

Hedging Options

If you want to reduce exposure to currency swings, consider using currency-hedged share classes (e.g. VUAG GBP-hedged) or adding positions in UK/GBP-denominated bond funds. These typically have slightly higher costs but smooth out FX volatility.
